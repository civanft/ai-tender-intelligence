Tender Meridian is an independent research dashboard for exploring AI, data analytics and data infrastructure opportunities across all 27 EU member states.

Explore the dashboard →

What you can explore

Compare markets, follow procurement trends and inspect individual notices with their buyer, budget, deadline and technology theme where those details are disclosed.

Where the data comes from

Public notices are collected from the EU Tenders Electronic Daily (TED) service. Each opportunity links back to its official source record.

How screening works

CPV codes and notice text identify possible AI and data work. An explainable opportunity score measures fit with a configured research profile; it is not a prediction of contract award.

How to use the results

Automated screening can miss relevant notices or include false matches. Source records may be incomplete or amended. Check the official TED notice before making a decision.

Tender Meridian is an independent research project and is not affiliated with the European Union or TED.
